SOS: Safra's budgeting system

Safra · 2020

Imagem · logo/foto do projetoScreenshot or diagram of the budgeting system

Problem. Each area planned its budget (office expenses up to travel, sponsorships and philanthropy) in Excel. About 30 budget "champions" sent files to the planning area, which validated, consolidated and reconciled everything by hand and sent errors back. Slow, manual, error-prone.

What we built. "SOS" (Sistema de Orçamento do Safra), a responsive web system on the bank's intranet. Users enter the budget with validation at input time, so there is no back-and-forth; the budget manager sees who filled what and when, sets calendars and deadlines, and approves; approved budgets flow to finance for release.

My role. Tech lead and backend developer. I coordinated a team of four (design, frontend, backend) and built the backend and the database with the other backend developer.

Result. The whole bank moved to it.

Tech: Angular (v9), Node.js with TypeORM, SQL database
